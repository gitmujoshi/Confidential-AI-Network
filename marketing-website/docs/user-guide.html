Getting Started

Welcome to ContractFlow Pro! This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get started with secure AI model training.

Before You Begin

Ensure you have a valid email address and understand your role in the AI training ecosystem. You'll need to choose between being a Training Data Provider (TDP), Training Data Consumer (TDC), Confidential Clean Room Provider (CCRP), or System Administrator.

Step 1: Register

Create your account and verify your email address

Step 2: Authenticate

Log in and set up your security preferences

Step 3: Choose Role

Select your role and configure permissions

Step 4: Start Using

Access your dashboard and begin working

User Registration

The registration process is designed to be secure and user-friendly while ensuring proper identity verification.

Registration Process
1. Account Creation
  • Visit the registration page and provide your email address
  • Create a strong password (minimum 12 characters with mixed case, numbers, and symbols)
  • Accept the terms of service and privacy policy
  • Complete the CAPTCHA verification
2. Email Verification
  • Check your email for a verification link
  • Click the link to verify your email address
  • Verification links expire after 24 hours
  • If expired, request a new verification email
3. Profile Setup
  • Complete your profile information (name, organization, contact details)
  • Upload profile picture (optional)
  • Set your timezone and language preferences
  • Configure notification settings
Important Notes
  • Use a valid, accessible email address for account recovery
  • Keep your password secure and don't share it with others
  • Complete email verification before proceeding with role selection
  • Contact support if you encounter any issues during registration

Authentication & Security

Secure authentication methods ensure only authorized users can access the platform and its features.

Login Process
Standard Login
  1. Enter your registered email address
  2. Provide your password
  3. Complete any required CAPTCHA
  4. Click "Sign In" to access your account
Security Features
  • Account lockout after 5 failed attempts
  • Session timeout after 30 minutes of inactivity
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all communications
  • Audit logging of all login attempts
Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA Setup
  1. Enable MFA in your security settings
  2. Scan QR code with authenticator app
  3. Enter the generated 6-digit code
  4. Save backup codes for account recovery
Supported Methods
  • Google Authenticator
  • Microsoft Authenticator
  • Authy
  • SMS verification (optional)
Password Security
Password Requirements
  • Minimum 12 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter
  • At least one lowercase letter
  • At least one number
  • At least one special character
Password Best Practices
  • Use unique passwords for each account
  • Consider using a password manager
  • Change passwords regularly (every 90 days)
  • Never share passwords via email or chat

Role Management

Understanding your role and its permissions is crucial for effective platform usage.

Training Data Provider (TDP)
Responsibilities
  • Upload and manage training datasets
  • Set licensing terms and pricing
  • Monitor dataset usage and revenue
  • Maintain data quality and documentation
Permissions
  • Create and manage dataset listings
  • Set access controls and restrictions
  • View analytics and usage reports
  • Manage licensing agreements
Training Data Consumer (TDC)
Responsibilities
  • Browse available datasets
  • Request data access and licensing
  • Train AI models in secure environments
  • Comply with usage agreements
Permissions
  • Search and filter datasets
  • Submit access requests
  • Monitor training progress
  • Download training results
Confidential Clean Room Provider (CCRP)
Responsibilities
  • Provide secure computing environments
  • Execute AI training jobs
  • Ensure data isolation and security
  • Monitor system performance
Permissions
  • Manage computing resources
  • Execute training workflows
  • Monitor security status
  • Generate audit reports
System Administrator
Responsibilities
  • Manage platform configuration
  • Monitor system health
  • Handle user support
  • Manage security policies
Permissions
  • Access all system areas
  • Manage user accounts and roles
  • Configure system settings
  • View system logs and analytics

Contract Signing Process

Ricardian contracts enable automated, legally enforceable agreements between platform participants.

Contract Lifecycle
1. Contract Creation

Who: Training Data Provider (TDP)

Action: Creates contract with dataset terms, pricing, and usage restrictions

  • Define dataset scope and limitations
  • Set pricing structure and payment terms
  • Specify usage restrictions and compliance requirements
  • Set contract duration and renewal terms
2. Contract Review

Who: Training Data Consumer (TDC)

Action: Reviews contract terms and accepts or negotiates modifications

  • Review dataset specifications and quality
  • Evaluate pricing and payment terms
  • Assess compliance with requirements
  • Request modifications if needed
3. Digital Signatures

Who: All parties (TDP, TDC, CCRP)

Action: Digitally sign the contract using authenticated identities

  • Verify identity through multi-factor authentication
  • Review final contract terms
  • Apply digital signature with timestamp
  • Receive confirmation of signature
4. Contract Execution

Who: System automatically

Action: Smart contracts automatically enforce terms and handle payments

  • Automated payment processing
  • Data access provisioning
  • Usage monitoring and compliance
  • Contract renewal notifications
Contract Types and Templates
Data Licensing Contracts

Standard templates for dataset licensing with customizable terms for:

  • Usage scope and limitations
  • Pricing and payment schedules
  • Data retention and deletion
  • Compliance requirements
Service Level Agreements

CCRP service agreements covering:

  • Computing resource availability
  • Security and compliance standards
  • Performance guarantees
  • Support and maintenance
Contract Security Features
  • Digital Signatures: Legally binding signatures with timestamp verification
  • Audit Trails: Complete record of all contract modifications and actions
  • Compliance Validation: Automatic checking against regulatory requirements
  • Dispute Resolution: Built-in mechanisms for handling disagreements
