Ethical AI Principles

Our commitment to responsible AI development.

Fairness

Ensure AI systems treat all individuals and groups fairly without discrimination.

Transparency

Provide clear explanations of how AI systems make decisions and operate.

Accountability

Establish clear responsibility for AI system outcomes and decisions.

Privacy

Protect individual privacy and maintain data security throughout AI development.

Safety

Ensure AI systems operate safely and do not cause harm to users or society.

Human Oversight

Maintain human control and oversight of AI systems and their decisions.
