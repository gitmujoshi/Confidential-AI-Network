### **Training Philosophy**
Our training program is designed to provide:
- **Role-Specific Training**: Tailored content for each user role
- **Hands-On Learning**: Practical exercises and real-world scenarios
- **Progressive Learning**: Step-by-step modules building on each other
- **Certification**: Professional certification for each role
- **Continuous Learning**: Ongoing training and updates

### **Training Delivery Methods**
- **Self-Paced Learning**: Complete modules at your own pace
- **Instructor-Led Training**: Scheduled training sessions with experts
- **Workshops**: Hands-on training workshops
- **Webinars**: Regular training webinars on specific topics
- **Video Tutorials**: Step-by-step video guides
